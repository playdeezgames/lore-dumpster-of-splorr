## Page text

**Latest version:** https://playdeezgames.github.io/lore-dumpster-of-splorr/

**Lore Dumpster of SPLORR!!** is a turn-based puzzle adventure in the look of the TRS-80 Color Computer's `PMODE 1`: 128 by 96 pixels and four colours. The Investors of SPLORR!! bought the other twelve. You descend the vault to get them back. The hardware says no.

**This is a demo: the first block of 10 levels.** Whether the rest gets built depends on whether people find it interesting, so if you play it, please tell us what you think (press F in the game, or leave a comment).

- Each level is one room: find the way to the stairs.
- Traps run on fixed cycles: spikes, arrow slots, fire jets, crushers, rolling boulders, pits, crumbling floors. Watch them a few turns before you walk in.
- Keys and doors, bombs for cracked walls and things that bite, fake walls hiding secrets, and one hidden gem in most levels.
- Restarting a level costs a life, and so does dying. Run out and it's over.
- Every level comes with a page of lore. Nobody asked for it.

### How to play

Arrows or WASD move, Space waits (or turns the page), B throws a bomb, R restarts the level (costs a life), Esc or N says no, F sends feedback. On a phone or tablet, on-screen pads appear on the first touch. A gamepad works too.
