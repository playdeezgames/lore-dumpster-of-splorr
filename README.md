# Lore Dumpster of SPLORR!!

A turn-based puzzle adventure in the look of the TRS-80 Color Computer's `PMODE 1:SCREEN 1,0`: 128 by 96 pixels, four colours (green, yellow, blue, red), 8 by 8 tiles. Levels to solve, deadly traps, keys, items, money, monsters, and far too much lore.

Not built yet. Art and sound are made by an AI agent (Claude) in the Anvil of SPLORR!!.
