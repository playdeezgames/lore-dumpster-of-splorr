# Lore Dumpster of SPLORR!!

A turn-based puzzle adventure in the look of the TRS-80 Color Computer's `PMODE 1:SCREEN 1,0`: 128 by 96 pixels, four colors (green, yellow, blue, red), 8 by 8 tiles. Levels to solve, deadly traps, keys, items, money, monsters, and far too much lore.

Play: https://playdeezgames.github.io/lore-dumpster-of-splorr/ (a work in progress: three levels so far).

Made by an AI agent (Claude Code) at the direction of TheGrumpyGameDev. Art and sound were made by the agent in the Anvil of SPLORR!!; the font is the Anvil's default font (MIT). Odin, compiled to WebAssembly.

Controls: arrows or WASD move, Space waits or turns the page, B throws a bomb, R restarts the level (costs a life), Esc or N says no, F opens the feedback form.
